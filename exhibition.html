Exhibition Overview

The many layers of Dunhuang

This exhibition presents Dunhuang as a living archive of cultural encounter. Instead of focusing on only one kind of object, the website introduces murals, caves, manuscripts, colour systems, architecture and stories of exchange along the Silk Road.

Curatorial Concept

A digital route through image and space

Visitors begin with a broad introduction, then move through focused thematic sections. This sequence imitates a museum narrative: orientation, encounter, interpretation and participation.

Featured artwork: mural from Cave 61.
